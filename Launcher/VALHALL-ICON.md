## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: final macOS app icon artwork for an
application named Valhall (Swedish spelling), square 1024 by 1024 composition.
Create one exceptionally polished, bold Viking Valhall emblem: a front-facing
ancient Norse war helmet with a strong angular T-shaped face opening, forged dark
iron and burnished gold edging, framed by two subtle raven-wing silhouettes and
a restrained ring of interlaced Norse knotwork. The helmet is the dominant simple
silhouette, powerful and dignified, a modern premium game launcher icon rather
than a detailed illustration. No horns. Dramatic icy blue inner glow and warm
forge-gold highlights on nearly black midnight steel. Sculpted depth, precise
bevels, restrained texture; high contrast and readable at tiny desktop icon
sizes. Present the emblem inside a clean, solid dark midnight-blue macOS
rounded-square tile with softly rounded edges and generous internal padding;
transparent pixels outside the rounded-square tile, authentic alpha transparency.
Head-on symmetrical centered composition. No words, letters, typography, runes,
logos, watermark, desktop mockup, scenery or additional panels.
